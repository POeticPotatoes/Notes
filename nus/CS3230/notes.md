 **Algorithms** are unambiguous and executable instructions for solving a problem

 Preferred characteristics
 * Correct
 * Efficient
 * General
 * Usable as a subroutine
 * Simple
 * Well Documented

## Iterative algorithm (Similar to induction)
 * Loop invariant (define goal of proof (value for each state))
 * Initialization (Base Case)
 * Maintenance (Induction step)
 * Termination (Conclusion)
